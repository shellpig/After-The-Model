A 2D side-scrolling video game background, 4768x896 resolution, strictly orthographic 2D side view, flat profile. The scene is the interior server array corridor of a massive, monumental AI data center.
Visual style: Flat cel-shaded indie comic art style, clean line art, no textures, no halftone, solid colors.
Composition & scaling: Symmetrical rows of massive, towering off-white and cool gray-blue server racks that rise up like monumental pillars, filling 80% of the vertical frame. At the bottom, a flat, clean, horizontal concrete floor for a walking path. Human scale is tiny compared to the massive machines.
Color palette: 70% sterile off-white and pale cold blue-white, 15% cool gray-blue for shadows and panel seams, 10% deep ink-black voids in the recessed server rack slots, 5% cyber-purple glowing LED indicator dots on the server panels. Strict rule: 0% warm colors (NO orange, NO amber, NO gold).
Foreground and midground details: Completely clean, clinical, and sterile. No trash, no graffiti, no cables on the floor. Only glowing cyber-purple status strips along the floor and servers. Left edge suggests a high-tech entrance; right edge leads deeper into the core.
