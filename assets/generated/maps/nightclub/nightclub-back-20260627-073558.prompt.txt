尺寸：3840 × 720 px（≈ 3 個螢幕寬，矮天花、私密）。
色彩聖經對齊 anchor-04（黑金 + cyber-purple 為主導且每包廂皆主導），每包廂另加「一抹」珠寶副色：①玫瑰酒紅 ②皇家紫羅蘭 ③翡翠綠。
重點：包廂之間不要大空格、把包廂做大填滿；三包廂座位風格/燈具/副色各不同；房內無人、奢華非破爛。

---

A 2D side-scrolling video game background. STRICTLY orthographic
side view, like Hollow Knight, The Last Night, Limbo. A
WEALTHY-DISTRICT BACKSTAGE INTERIOR — a run of EXACTLY THREE LARGE,
opulent VIP "real conversation" private booths along a plush
backstage corridor. HIGH LUXURY + CYBERPUNK, pristine and intact,
intimate and dim-but-RICH, EMPTY OF PEOPLE. Same 富人區 palette as
anchor-04 (near-pure cold ink black + GLOWING gold/champagne +
cyber-purple) — which stays DOMINANT in every booth — but EACH of
the three booths has its OWN distinct style and ONE small secondary
jewel-tone accent.

## CANVAS
- 3840 × 720 px. A LONG corridor (~3 screens). LOW ceiling —
  intimate, private, lavish (not cramped-shabby).

## ★ THREE LARGE BOOTHS, TIGHT SPACING (CRITICAL — the fix) ★
EXACTLY THREE booths. Make each booth BIG — they should fill most
of the frame width. The corridor walls BETWEEN booths are NARROW
(just a slim marble pier / divider, ~4-6% width each), NOT wide
empty gaps. Distribution across the 3840 width roughly:
  left entry ~10% | BOOTH 1 ~24% | pier | BOOTH 2 ~24% | pier |
  BOOTH 3 ~24% | right vanity ~10%.
Each booth is a generous alcove the player clearly walks past.

## ★ THREE DISTINCT BOOTHS (each its own style + ONE accent color) ★
Black + gold + cyber-purple stays DOMINANT in all three; the
secondary color is a SUBTLE single accent (tinting the velvet
upholstery + the booth's holo-screen glow + a thread in the neon
trim), never overpowering gold/black.

- BOOTH 1 (left) — "LOUNGE": a long curved button-tufted velvet
  BANQUETTE wrapping the alcove + low marble table with gold-lit
  champagne glasses. Ceiling: a cascading linear gold pendant.
  Secondary accent = DEEP ROSE / WINE (rosé-tinted velvet + a
  faint rose tint in its holo-screen and trim).

- BOOTH 2 (center) — "INTIMATE FACE-TO-FACE": a pair of plush
  barrel/tub armchairs facing across a round marble table with a
  single gold-lit decanter. Ceiling: a circular gold halo
  chandelier. Secondary accent = ROYAL VIOLET / deeper cyber-
  purple (violet-tinted velvet + a stronger purple holo glow).

- BOOTH 3 (right) — "RECLINE": a chaise-longue + a single accent
  chair + a low ottoman, more relaxed asymmetric arrangement.
  Ceiling: a cluster of suspended small gold orbs. Secondary
  accent = EMERALD / JADE GREEN (emerald-tinted velvet + a jade
  tint in its holo-screen and trim).

All three booths share: GLOWING brushed-gold neon trim around the
alcove mouth, a softly GLOWING privacy holo-screen on the back
wall (sleek panel with abstract glyphs, NO readable text, tinted
by that booth's accent — NOT a dead black rectangle), pristine
luxury, NO people.

## ENDS
- LEFT EDGE (~10%): entry from lobby — a sumptuous deep velvet
  drape pulled aside, framed by glowing brushed-gold trim
  (from_lobby spawn).
- RIGHT EDGE (~10%): an elegant vanity / host station — a large
  gold-framed mirror with a backlit warm gold light bar (clean,
  reflective) + a sleek dark concierge terminal with a live
  CYBER-PURPLE standby glow.

## VERTICAL LAYOUT
- BOTTOM 16-18%: polished dark marble + rich velvet carpet runner
  walking floor (thin strip, CLEAR horizontal walk line, faint
  gold reflections)
- REMAINING 82-84%: booth openings + back wall — NON-PLAYABLE.
  Player moves left-right only.

## SCALE REFERENCE
Implied human scale ~30-32% of this shorter frame (furniture sized
to it), but NO figures drawn. Each booth opening ~58-62% of frame
height.

## VIEW RULES (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
- ORTHOGRAPHIC ONLY. No perspective, no vanishing point. Floor and
  ceiling perfectly horizontal.
- Booths shown as FLAT side-profile alcoves — NO receding interior
  depth, NO 3/4 view, like luxury stage boxes head-on.
- Every object a FLAT CARDBOARD CUTOUT. Floor strip stays clear.

## COMPOSITION DISCIPLINE (DIM, PRIVATE, LAVISH, GLOWING)
- ~45% near-pure cold ink black (booth recesses, low ceiling,
  shadowed piers — clean deep dark, not grimy)
- ~20% rich midtones (velvet upholstery, polished dark marble,
  warm gold-lit mirror)
- ~35% bright & GLOWING: champagne/brushed-GOLD that GLOWS (neon
  trim, light fixtures, gold-lit drinks, mirror bar, floor
  reflections) + CYBER-PURPLE holo glow + each booth's small
  jewel accent (rose / violet / jade). Gold is LIVE and lustrous,
  never tarnished.
- Mood: an exclusive private lounge after hours — opulent, hushed,
  cyber-lit, waiting. Empty = "no one here right now," NOT
  "fell apart."

## STRICT DO NOT
- DO NOT make it look run-down, shabby, broken, abandoned. NO dust
  sheets, NO ripped/tipped/broken furniture, NO cracked mirrors,
  NO fallen ropes, NO trash, NO grime. PRISTINE LUXURY.
- DO NOT use wide empty gaps between booths — piers are slim,
  booths are large and close.
- More or fewer than THREE booths = WRONG. EXACTLY THREE, each a
  different style + accent.
- DO NOT let any booth's secondary accent overpower black/gold —
  it is a subtle single tint only.
- DO NOT draw ANY human figure or silhouette anywhere.
- DO NOT use dead flat-black screens — privacy screens softly GLOW.
- DO NOT make the gold dull, tarnished, or desaturated — gold
  GLOWS.
- NO readable text — abstract glyphs only.
- NO perspective, NO side walls, NO receding interiors, NO 3/4
  view.
- NO pure white, NO pure black (~2% off). NO cyan, NO cool-white
  LED — AI tech is CYBER PURPLE only.
- NO faded amber / burnt orange / rust (那是平民區). The jewel
  accents are rose-wine / royal-violet / emerald — NOT orange,
  NOT cyan.
- NO ink purple-black (那是平民區的黑) — near-pure cold ink black.
- NO graffiti, posters, exposed pipes.
- NO anime lighting, photorealism, 3D render look.
- Floor strip must NOT exceed 18% of frame height.

## COMIC TECHNIQUE
American indie cyberpunk comic (HD 2D, NOT pixel art). Mike Mignola
heavy black for the deep booth recesses (rich negative space, not
grime). Frank Miller contrast. Sean Murphy glossy corporate-luxury
interiors. Geof Darrow precise linework for the holo-screens,
fixtures, and terminal. Hand-inked clean SHARP lines. Halftone
light and sparse (glossy magazine). Flat cel-shade, no gradients.
