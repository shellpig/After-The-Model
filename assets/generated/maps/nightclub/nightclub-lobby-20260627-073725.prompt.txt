A 2D side-scrolling video game background. STRICTLY orthographic
side view, like Hollow Knight, The Last Night, Limbo. A
WEALTHY-DISTRICT INTERIOR — the ground-floor LOBBY / BAR of an
upscale nightclub. It is glamorous, expensive, and high-tech, BUT
COMPLETELY EMPTY OF PEOPLE. Same 富人區 palette as anchor-04
(near-pure cold ink black + GLOWING gold/champagne + cyber-purple),
warm and lavish, with STRONG CYBERPUNK tech presence.

## CANVAS
- 3840 × 896 px. A WIDE interior hall (~3 screens). Tall ceiling
  to sell luxury height. Keep the already-approved layout below.

## ★ NO PEOPLE RULE (CRITICAL) ★
There are NO human figures anywhere in this image — NO patrons,
NO bartender, NO silhouettes, NO dark human shapes, NO bodies of
any kind, in foreground, midground, OR background. The room reads
as "an opulent bar that is open but momentarily empty." The sense
of life/occupancy comes ENTIRELY from SET DRESSING and TECH, not
from people:
- gold-lit drink glasses left on tables (recently used)
- softly lit, slightly-disturbed plush seating
- live glowing screens and holograms
- hovering service DRONES (machines, not people)
Absolutely do NOT paint any human silhouette to suggest a crowd.

## ★ CYBERPUNK ELEMENTS (add these — the upgrade) ★
- A floating CYBER-PURPLE holographic "real conversation" price
  projection hovering above the bar or beside the menu board
  (abstract glyphs, NO readable text, thin scanline shimmer)
- Holographic gold/purple bottle labels or a rotating holo drink
  display floating above the bar counter
- ONE or TWO small sleek HOVER SERVICE DRONES drifting in the
  lounge (dark chrome bodies, a gold tray or a cyber-purple sensor
  ring, faint thruster glow) — the "staff" of this place is
  automated
- Thin GOLD + CYBER-PURPLE LED light channels traced along the
  architecture: floor-embedded strips, column edges, ceiling
  coffers, booth outlines — sleek neon piping
- A faint holographic brand sigil shimmering in front of / over
  the big gold emblem (animated AR layer feel)
- A subtle cyber-purple ambient data-mist / volumetric glow near
  the ceiling tech, signalling pervasive AI presence
- Keep these tasteful and surgical — luxury cyberpunk, not arcade
  clutter

## VIEW RULES (HIGHEST PRIORITY — VIOLATION = FAIL)
- ORTHOGRAPHIC ONLY. No perspective, no vanishing point, no
  foreshortening. Floor and ceiling perfectly horizontal.
- Camera EXACTLY perpendicular. ONLY flat front faces. NO side
  walls, NO receding floor, NO 3/4 view.
- Every object & hologram is a FLAT CARDBOARD CUTOUT / flat layer.

## COMIC TECHNIQUE
American indie cyberpunk comic (HD 2D, NOT pixel art). Mike Mignola
heavy-black mastery (marble walls as black masses, emblem as
negative space). Frank Miller Sin City contrast. Sean Murphy
corporate-luxury interiors. Geof Darrow precise linework for the
holograms and drones. Moebius clean geometry for the emblem. Hand-
inked SHARP, clean lines. Halftone LIGHTER and sparser (glossy
magazine). Flat cel-shade, no gradients.
