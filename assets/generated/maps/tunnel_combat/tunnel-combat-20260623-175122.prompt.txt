## tunnel_combat — Phase 18-A 廢棄通道戰鬥場景背景
## 生圖目標：3 螢幕寬（3840×720, 16:3）；實際採用原生輸出 4768×896（同 16:3 比例）
## 場景採用值：MAP_WIDTH := 4768.0 / CAMERA_Y 536 / limit_bottom 896（對齊 subway_station_platform 慣例：用圖真實尺寸）
## 風格錨點：與 anchor-03（地鐵 B1）同世界，但更暗、更廢棄、機械感更重

A 2D side-scrolling video game background, EXTRA-WIDE panorama (16:3 aspect,
~5.3:1, three 1280×720 screens wide, meant to scroll horizontally
left-to-right). STRICTLY orthographic side view, like Hollow Knight, The Last
Night, Limbo, or Ranx (1990). Matching the visual style of anchor-01,
anchor-02, anchor-03 in /assets/art_bible/ — same world as the subway
concourse, but this is a DERELICT, ABANDONED maintenance tunnel deeper
underground.

## SCENE FRAMING
The interior of a long abandoned service/utility tunnel fills the ENTIRE
frame, edge to edge. Floor, ceiling, and back wall extend off both the LEFT
and RIGHT edges — the corridor continues beyond the frame on both sides.
NO external structure, NO "outside the tunnel". This is a closed underground
space.

## OVERALL TONE (CRITICAL — this is a DARK scene)
This tunnel is mostly DARKNESS. Heavy Mignola black dominates — ceiling,
upper walls, and most corners are deep purple-black solid shadow. Light
exists only in a FEW small isolated POOLS, with long stretches of near-dark
between them. The mood is cold, derelict, forgotten — power has mostly been
cut, only a few fixtures still cling to life. Roughly:
- ~60-65% deep shadow (ceiling, upper walls, dead stretches between lights)
- ~25-30% midtones (the lit wall/floor sections, exposed concrete)
- ~8-10% bright (only the few working light sources + small accents)

## HORIZONTAL LAYOUT (three screens, scrolling)
Because the frame is THREE screens wide, distribute interest across the
length with clear DARK GAPS between lit beats (this rhythm of light/dark is
the whole point):

- LEFT THIRD — TUNNEL MOUTH / ENTRANCE (the way back to the settlement):
  A reinforced doorway / breached bulkhead on the far left where the player
  enters from the settlement's right room. One dim, slightly warm amber bulb
  still works above it (faded interior comfort warm — "this is where people
  still are"). Just past it the light dies into shadow.

- CENTER THIRD — THE COMBAT ARENA (open breathing space):
  A WIDE, mostly EMPTY stretch of clear walking floor — this is where the
  fight happens, keep it uncluttered. ONE working maintenance lamp casts a
  single cold cyan-white pool of light onto the floor here (flickering,
  buzzing), the rest fades to dark at the edges. Props stay pinned to the
  back wall / ceiling, never on the floor.

- RIGHT THIRD — DEAD END / LOST-PROPERTY (post-combat reward zone):
  The tunnel terminates in a collapsed / sealed dead end. Against the back
  wall sits ONE battered metal lost-property / logistics crate (a small
  freight locker, lid closed) — the only notable floor object in the whole
  scene, lit by a weak failing light so the player's eye finds it after the
  fight. Faint cyan indicator still blinking on it.

## VERTICAL LAYOUT (CRITICAL)
- BOTTOM 15-18% of frame: the walking floor — a thin horizontal strip, the
  ONLY playable surface. Keep it clear for left-right walking and combat.
- REMAINING 82-85%: back wall, ceiling, pipes, machinery — all NON-PLAYABLE
  visual context, mostly swallowed in black.
The player must NEVER feel they can move up or down. Floor is flat, thin,
horizontal. Do NOT make the floor more than ~18% of frame height.

## VIEW RULES
- ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION ONLY. No perspective, no vanishing point.
- Floor and ceiling are perfectly horizontal lines.
- The tunnel does NOT recede into depth — it is a flat side cross-section.
- Every object is a CARDBOARD CUTOUT in pure side profile, flat against the
  wall. NO 3/4 view of anything.

## SCENE CONTENT — back wall & ceiling (upper 82%, mostly dark)
- Exposed concrete with cracks, water stains, rust streaks, peeling paint —
  "used hard, then abandoned".
- Thick bundles of exposed pipes and dead conduit running along the ceiling
  edge, sagging, some severed.
- A few DEAD / DARK light fixtures (broken fluorescent tubes, shattered
  housings) — emphasizing that most lights are OUT.
- Defunct wall machinery / a rusted junction box / an old service panel,
  hanging open, wires out.
- Optional: faint silhouette of a non-working ventilation fan high in shadow.
- Hand-inked grime, drips, debris piles at the base of the back wall.

## LIGHT SOURCES (only THREE, isolated)
1. LEFT: dim warm amber bulb over the entrance doorway (faded comfort warm).
2. CENTER: one cold cyan-white maintenance lamp, flickering, single floor
   pool — the combat light.
3. RIGHT: one weak failing light near the dead-end crate, plus the crate's
   small blinking cyan indicator.
Everything between and above these is deep purple-black shadow.

## ★ ORANGE SCALE RULE (CRITICAL) ★
Saturated burnt orange is the NARRATIVE MARKER COLOR of 平民區 — the mark of
PEOPLE who passed through, never the system. It loses meaning if spread.
HARD LIMITS:
- TOTAL orange ≤ 1-2% of all pixels. Not 5%, not 10%.
- Orange is always a SHAPE (a tag, a stripe, a stencil), NEVER a fill, NEVER
  on machinery or fixtures.
- MAXIMUM TWO orange spots:
  (1) PRIMARY: one small burnt-orange spray-painted arrow or tag near the
      entrance — a maintenance worker's old route mark, "someone came this
      way".
  (2) SUPPORTING: one tiny orange sticker / hazard stencil on the dead-end
      crate or a pipe.
Anything else orange = wrong.

## Comic Technique
American indie cyberpunk comic (HD 2D, NOT pixel art). Heavy Mignola black
for ceiling, dead stretches and corners — use "not drawing" to tell the
story of an abandoned place. Frank Miller chiaroscuro: the few light pools
read as bright ONLY because everything around them is black. Paul Pope
hand-inked varied-weight linework. Halftone dots in midtones only (heavy,
riso zine grain). Flat cel-shade, hard shadow edges, NO gradients.

## Color
Overall: deep teal, gray-blue, ink purple-black — cold, institutional,
underground, dead. Warm accent only at the entrance bulb (faded amber, low
saturation, interior comfort warm — NOT the focal orange). Neon: cold
cyan-white on the working maintenance lamp and the crate indicator; a touch
of magenta is acceptable on a dead indicator. The ONLY fully-saturated warm
color is the burnt-orange worker mark (≤2%).

## STRICT DO NOT
- DO NOT make it brightly or evenly lit — most of the frame is BLACK.
- DO NOT light the whole tunnel; light is in 3 isolated pools only.
- DO NOT make the floor more than ~18% of frame height.
- DO NOT clutter the central combat floor — keep it open.
- DO NOT use perspective or let the tunnel recede into depth.
- DO NOT show 3/4 view of any object.
- DO NOT add NPCs or the cleaning machine (characters are separate sprites).
- DO NOT add readable text — all signage / stencils are abstract glyphs.
- DO NOT put orange on machinery, pipes, fixtures, or lights — only on the
  two worker-made marks.
- DO NOT let orange exceed ~2% of the frame or spread beyond two spots.
- DO NOT confuse the entrance amber glow with the focal orange.
- DO NOT use pure white (lightest = faded cream) or pure black (darkest =
  deep purple-black).
- DO NOT use gold or brass (that's 富人區).
- DO NOT use saturated cyber-UV-violet (that's the AI district).
- DO NOT make it look futuristic-clean; it is derelict and grimy.
- DO NOT add anime lighting, photorealism, or 3D render look.
