A 2D side-scrolling video game background. STRICTLY orthographic
side view, like Hollow Knight, The Last Night, Limbo, or Ranx (1990).
Matching the visual style of anchor-01, anchor-02, anchor-03 in
/assets/art_bible/.

## CANVAS
- Aspect ratio ~4.4:1 (3200 x 720), a wide horizontal panel, about
  2.5 screens wide. This is the RIGHT HALF of a larger settlement
  map; it must tile seamlessly on its LEFT edge to the left panel
  (same floor height, same tunnel ceiling height at the seam).
- The walking floor is a SINGLE continuous horizontal line across
  the ENTIRE width, level end to end.

## SCENE — Underground homeless SETTLEMENT (cold "infrastructure /
## threat" side)
The same storm-drain / maintenance tunnel settlement as the left
panel, but here it shifts from the warm tent-home to the COLD
INFRASTRUCTURE that keeps it alive — and toward the dark deeper
tunnel that threatens it. Theme = WARM HUMANITY vs COLD
INFRASTRUCTURE, tilting cold: jury-rigged machines that actually
RUN (the settlement has scrappy working power, unlike the dead
subway above), an open communal clearing, and an ominous sealed
tunnel mouth leading deeper. Battered, humble, working, watchful.
IMPORTANT: NO people, NO figures, NO NPCs in frame. Convey life and
labor ONLY through machines, tools, traces, and light. The central
clearing is deliberately EMPTY (reserved open space).

## SCENE FRAMING
The tunnel INTERIOR fills the ENTIRE frame. Floor, low concrete
tunnel ceiling, and back wall extend to all edges. NO sky, NO
"outside." Orthographic — no perspective, no vanishing point;
floor and ceiling read as horizontal. Converted drainage bore:
slightly arched concrete ceiling kept flat/orthographic, exposed
pipes and cable runs along it.

## HORIZONTAL LAYOUT

- LEFT EDGE (~0-12%): OPEN tunnel continuation from the left panel
  — the dwelling area fading out, a last bit of pipe/cable/wall and
  maybe one crate. NOT a capped wall; the tunnel flows in from the
  left so the seam is invisible. Floor and ceiling height MUST match
  the left panel exactly.

- LEFT-CENTER (~12-42%): the WATER & POWER STATION — a jury-rigged
  rig built from RETROFITTED OLD MACHINES: scavenged tanks, a
  patched water-purifier with hoses and drip filters, a wheezing
  generator unit, gauges, valves, welded patches, taped cables,
  condensation and slow drips. It is FUNCTIONING but humble: a few
  faint working indicators (small cyan/amber gauge glints), a thin
  trickle of clean water, a low mechanical presence. TRACES of a
  repairer (no person): a work lamp casting one small warm pool of
  light, a toolbox, a wrench left on a crate, a rag. This is the
  cold body that warms the home.

- CENTER (~42-66%): the CENTRAL CLEARING — a communal open space,
  deliberately EMPTY and clear (reserved gathering ground). Just
  worn tunnel floor, a couple of crates or a low bench at the
  edges, faint scuff marks where people gather. Quiet, a bit empty,
  "everyone has stepped away." Keep this zone OPEN and uncluttered.

- CENTER-RIGHT (~66-76%): the RADIO — ONE old salvaged radio /
  battered speaker on a crate or wall shelf, a single weak indicator
  glow, a thin antenna. It is the faint source of a soft ambient
  hum/song drifting through the tunnel. Small prop, not dominant.

- FAR RIGHT (~76-100%): the DEEPER TUNNEL MOUTH — the dark opening
  leading further into the drains, the END of the map. A large dark
  archway / tunnel mouth, SEALED FOR NOW: a makeshift barricade —
  a welded grate, chained boards, stacked debris, or a pulled-down
  shutter — blocking passage. Beyond/through it: cold blue-black
  darkness, a faint draft, an ominous depth. It reads as "danger /
  closed off, do not go deeper yet." Cold, shadowed, watchful. This
  is the RIGHT END of the whole map (capped here by the tunnel
  mouth).

## VERTICAL LAYOUT (CRITICAL)
- BOTTOM 15-20% of frame: the walking floor (thin horizontal strip
  / walkway), with a shallow dry drainage channel just in front.
- REMAINING 80-85%: tunnel wall, low ceiling, pipes, machines,
  tunnel mouth — all NON-PLAYABLE visual context.
- The player must NEVER feel they can move up or down in the floor
  area. Floor is a thin strip for left-right walking only. DO NOT
  let the floor exceed 20% of frame height.

## SCALE REFERENCE
A standing adult human would be ~22-25% of frame height. The water/
power rig is human-to-slightly-taller scale. Radio, toolbox, crates
are small human-scaled props. The deeper tunnel mouth is large
(taller than a person) but its opening stays within the upper
non-playable band.

## VIEW & FLAT PROFILE
- ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION ONLY. No perspective. No vanishing point.
- Every object is a CARDBOARD CUTOUT against the tunnel wall — flat
  side profile. Back wall is a flat backdrop.
- Floor stays CLEAR for a continuous horizontal walking path.

## "TRACES NOT PEOPLE" + "RESERVED EMPTY SPACE" (Phase requirement)
- NO human figures, NO NPCs, NO silhouettes anywhere.
- The central clearing must stay OPEN and empty — it is reserved
  space, not decorated with stand-in characters or props that
  imply a specific person.
- The deeper tunnel is SEALED/closed in this version — no enemies,
  no combat, no open passage. Just the blocked, ominous mouth.
- Convey life only through working machines, tools, and the radio.

## ★ ORANGE SCALE RULE (CRITICAL) ★
Saturated burnt orange is the NARRATIVE MARKER COLOR of the
plebeian / deleted underclass — "human will refusing to be
forgotten." It must always be a SMALL ACCENT SHAPE, never a fill.
HARD LIMITS:
- TOTAL orange ≤ 1-2% of frame pixels. Not 5%, not 10%.
- Maximum TWO orange spots:
  (1) [PRIMARY] ONE small hand-painted burnt-orange WARNING mark /
      symbol on the wall beside the sealed deeper-tunnel mouth — a
      resident's "danger, don't go deeper" scrawl.
  (2) [SUPPORTING] ONE small orange detail at the water/power
      station — a marked valve, a tagged tool, or a painted stripe.
- Orange is ALWAYS a SHAPE, NEVER a background fill, NEVER on bare
  pipes or structural concrete.
- DO NOT confuse the small warm work-lamp glow with the focal
  orange — the lamp is faded interior-comfort warmth, the orange is
  a tiny saturated narrative SHAPE.

## COMPOSITION DISCIPLINE
- ~50% deep shadow (tunnel ceiling, dim corners, the dark sealed
  tunnel mouth on the right, unlit machine recesses).
- ~38% midtones (concrete walls, floor, machine bodies).
- ~12% bright: faint cyan/amber machine indicators, the one small
  warm work-lamp pool at the station, the weak radio glow, and the
  ~1-2% orange accents.
- Cooler and more shadowed than the warm left panel; brightness
  drops toward the dark tunnel mouth on the far right. Most of the
  frame is dim, mechanical, watchful.

## STRICT DO NOT
- DO NOT draw any people, figures, NPCs, or human silhouettes.
- DO NOT cap or wall off the LEFT edge — the tunnel flows in from
  the left panel; the seam must be invisible (matching floor +
  ceiling height).
- DO NOT leave the deeper tunnel mouth OPEN or passable — it is
  sealed/barricaded in this version (no enemies, no combat).
- DO NOT clutter or fill the central clearing — keep it open/empty.
- DO NOT make it look clean, futuristic, or brightly lit — the
  machines are scavenged, patched, humble.
- DO NOT make the floor more than 20% of frame height.
- DO NOT use perspective lines or 3/4 views.
- DO NOT add readable text — any marks are abstract shapes/symbols.
- DO NOT let orange exceed ~2% or spread beyond two spots.
- DO NOT use pure white (lightest = faded cream) or pure black
  (darkest = deep purple-black).
- DO NOT use gold or brass tones (that's the wealthy district).
- DO NOT use saturated cyber-UV-violet (that's the AI district).

## COMIC TECHNIQUE
American indie cyberpunk comic (HD 2D, NOT pixel art). Mike Mignola
heavy black for tunnel ceiling, the deep tunnel mouth, and dim
corners. Frank Miller contrast. Paul Pope linework. Hand-inked
varied-weight lines. Halftone dots in midtones only. Flat cel-shade,
no gradients.

## COLOR
Overall base: deep teal, gray-blue, ink purple-black — cold,
underground concrete, more shadowed than the warm left panel.
Working-machine accents: faint cyan and amber gauge/indicator
glints (scrappy power that actually runs). ONE small warm work-lamp
pool at the water/power station (interior-comfort warm, a humble
spot of warmth amid the cold machinery). The deeper tunnel mouth on
the right is the darkest, coldest point — blue-black shadow.
Signature accent: SATURATED BURNT ORANGE — one warning mark by the
sealed tunnel mouth [primary] + one small marked-valve/tool detail
at the station [supporting], ≤2% of frame total. No pure white, no
pure black, no gold/brass, no saturated UV-violet.
