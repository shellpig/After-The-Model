An old personal photograph, rendered as a flat cel-shaded indie
comic illustration — NOT a real photograph, NOT photorealistic.
This image exists inside a 2D cyberpunk game as a "found photo"
artifact: a digital remnant recovered from erased data. It will
be displayed as a full-screen overlay when the player completes
collecting all fragments of this echo.

Resolution: 512x384 (4:3 landscape, old photo proportions).

## SUBJECT

A middle-aged person standing at the doorway of a small
apartment, seen from inside the hallway looking in. The door
has a room number plate reading "401" — this is the KEY
identifier and must be clearly legible.

The person:
- Age ~50-60, ordinary appearance, not glamorous — a regular
  tenant, someone's neighbor
- Standing in the doorway, one hand on the door frame, the
  other holding a cardboard box or a bundle of belongings
- Expression: quiet defiance mixed with resignation — they
  are being moved out but chose this, they refused to
  digitize their life
- Dressed simply: a worn jacket, plain clothes, nothing
  flashy — plebeian district resident
- Looking back over their shoulder into the apartment one
  last time, or facing the camera directly with a tired
  calm stare

The doorway / apartment glimpse:
- Behind or around the person: a sliver of a small lived-in
  apartment interior — a bookshelf edge, a window with
  light, a plant, something personal
- The hallway side (foreground): bare, institutional — cold
  fluorescent light, concrete or painted wall
- The contrast between the warm apartment interior (behind)
  and the cold hallway (in front) tells the story: leaving
  warmth for nowhere

## PHOTO ARTIFACT FEEL

This is not a clean modern digital image. It should feel like
a recovered analog photo that has been digitized — data
corruption and age coexist:

- Slightly faded overall — colors desaturated as if printed
  on old paper, then scanned
- Soft vignette at edges (darker corners) — old camera lens
- A few subtle scan artifacts: a faint horizontal line or
  two, a tiny color shift in one corner — suggesting this
  was pulled from corrupted storage
- Edges slightly rough or torn — the original print was
  handled many times
- Warm color cast overall (faded amber tint) — old film

## ★ RENDERING STYLE ★

CRITICAL: This must match the game's art style, not look like
a real photograph. It is a GAME ASSET, drawn in the same visual
language as the rest of the world:

- HAND-INKED BLACK OUTLINES on the person, doorframe, objects
  — thick outer, thin inner, organic brush-pen wobble
- FLAT CEL-SHADE coloring: each surface 2-3 tones max,
  hard-edged shadow transitions
- HALFTONE DOT PATTERN in midtone areas — moderate density,
  plebeian district level (riso zine feel)
- Mignola BLACK MASSES in deep shadows (hallway corners,
  under the person's chin, shadow side of the box)
- The "old photo" qualities (fading, vignette, scan lines)
  are LAYERED ON TOP of the comic art style — not replacing
  it

Think: a hand-drawn comic illustration that has been printed,
crumpled, stained, scanned, and partially corrupted — not a
photograph that has been "cartoonified."

## COLOR

Plebeian district palette, further desaturated by age:

- Dominant: faded deep teal (hallway wall), faded cream /
  warm gray (apartment interior glimpse)
- Person's clothes: faded olive / gray-blue / dark brown —
  nothing bright
- Warm accent: faded amber from the apartment interior light
  leaking through the doorway — the only warmth in the image
- Door / frame: dark wood brown or institutional gray
- "401" plate: small metal plate, dark numerals on faded
  cream — must be readable

NO saturated orange (not a narrative marker — this is a found
photo, not an environment object).
NO bright colors, NO neon, NO cyber purple.
NO pure white, NO pure black.

Overall color mood: "a warm memory going cold" — the apartment
side is warmer, the hallway side is colder, the person stands
at the border.
