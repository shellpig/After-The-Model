A half-body character sprite for a 2D side-scrolling cyberpunk
game NPC. Single character, SEATED POSE, visible from WAIST UP
only. SOLID flat magenta background (#FF00FF — NO gradients, NO
scene, NO furniture, NO desk, NO chair, NO shadow, NO context
— pure single-color chroma backdrop for compositing onto an
existing game map).

This character is 鹿其琛 (Lu Qichen), known as 鹿三爺 — the
third young master of the ancient Lu clan, a wealthy old-money
family. Age ~45-50. A collector who sits behind his desk all
day appraising pre-AI era relics. He has a leg injury and
rarely stands. This sprite will be placed behind a desk in
his room scene — only the upper body will be visible in-game.

## WHAT TO DRAW

- WAIST UP ONLY — cut off naturally below the waist / at
  hip level, as if the rest is behind a desk
- Seated posture: torso slightly forward-leaning, like
  examining an object on a surface in front of him
- One arm forward / slightly extended (resting on an unseen
  desk surface), the other relaxed at side
- Head turned very slightly — 3/4 profile view (~30° off
  frontal), looking downward at something
- The frame should show him from roughly hip level to the
  top of his head, with a little breathing room above

## CHARACTER DESIGN

Male, ~45-50, medium-lean build. East-Asian features.
Distinguished, composed, quietly authoritative.

FACE:
- Calm, appraising downward gaze — absorbed in examining
- Thin metal-frame glasses: DARK GOLD / antique brass,
  round or half-rim, old-fashioned scholarly style
- Hair: graying dark, swept back neatly with texture and
  slight wave, a few loose strands at the temple
- Clean-shaven or very faint stubble
- Lines around eyes, slight hollow cheekbones — age, not
  neglect
- Face = ~10 hand-drawn ink marks on flat skin (Art Bible
  rule): eyes as small dark ink shapes behind glasses,
  mouth a short ink line, nose a few suggestive lines

OUTFIT (visible portion — waist up):
- ★ NEAR-BLACK LONG VEST over a DARK IVORY SHIRT ★
- The vest: deep ink black with subtle cold blue undertone,
  structured but draping with weight (quality wool or heavy
  silk), 5-6 small DARK GOLD / ANTIQUE BRASS buttons down
  the front (old, patinated, NOT shiny), subtle welt pocket
  at chest
- The shirt: mandarin collar OR classic spread collar,
  dark ivory / aged cream color, fine cotton or linen,
  long sleeves visible from the vest's armholes, cuffs
  neat with possible small dark gold cufflinks
- ★ A thin DARK GOLD WATCH CHAIN — a subtle curved line
  from vest pocket to a button ★

## ★ RENDERING STYLE (CRITICAL) ★

FLAT CEL-SHADED INDIE COMIC BOOK style. Must match the
existing game map he will be composited onto.

- Bold HAND-INKED BLACK OUTLINES: thick outer silhouette,
  thin inner lines, hand-drawn organic wobble
- FLAT CEL-SHADE: each surface AT MOST 3 tones (base +
  shadow + occasional highlight), HARD-EDGED transitions
- HALFTONE DOT PATTERN in midtone areas — lighter/sparser
  than commoner characters (wealthy district = refined)
- Mignola HEAVY BLACK for deepest shadow shapes (under
  chin, vest folds, arm undersides)
- NO gradients, NO airbrush, NO soft shading, NO
  photorealistic textures

STYLE REFERENCES:
- Mike Mignola's Hellboy character panels
- Paul Pope's THB character art
- Sean Murphy's Tokyo Ghost inked figures
- Streets of Rage 4 character sprites

## ★ COLOR (WEALTHY DISTRICT — NO ORANGE) ★

- VEST: near-pure cold ink black (~2% off true black)
- SHIRT: dark ivory / aged cream (NOT white)
- BUTTONS + WATCH CHAIN + GLASSES FRAME: dark gold /
  antique brass (small accents, patinated, NOT shiny)
- SKIN: warm muted tan
- HAIR: graying dark (salt-and-pepper, more gray at temples)

★ ZERO ORANGE of any kind — no burnt orange, no amber
  accents, no rust, no warm red-orange. Orange belongs to
  the protagonist. This character is old money. ★

BACKGROUND: pure SOLID #FF00FF magenta. Nothing else.
